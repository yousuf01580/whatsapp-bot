Privacy Policy

This WhatsApp chatbot answers messages automatically. This page explains what data it uses.

What we collect

When you message the bot on WhatsApp, we receive your phone number and the text of your message.

How we use it

Your message text is sent to Google's Gemini AI service to generate a reply. We may store your phone number and chat history in our database so the bot can remember the conversation.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with the services needed to run the bot: Meta (WhatsApp), Google (Gemini AI), Vercel (hosting) and MongoDB Atlas (database).

Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, email us and we will remove it.
